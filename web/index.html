Not included: recommendation engine that finds qualifying patients

What “candidate review” means

Plain language boundary

Trial Relay may help people review

Show exact criteria, open source records, highlight missing information, track the human review, and route a packet.

Trial Relay may show a trial-team outcome

After the authorised site decides, display its confirmed eligible/ineligible outcome with source and date.

Trial Relay may not decide who qualifies

No EMR-wide scan, inferred eligibility, recommendation score, “best trial,” or automatic patient list.

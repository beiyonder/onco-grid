How to use Trial Relay

Follow one case from the EMR to a trial-site answer. Every important choice stays with a person.

Usage guide

One clear path, three human owners

The oncologist chooses what to review. The coordinator checks the site and manages the handoff. The trial team makes the final eligibility decision.

Automatic patient matching is not active.The safe workflow helps people review evidence and complete the handoff. It never tells a patient or doctor that someone qualifies.

What the system does not do

The trial team still decides formal eligibility

Not included: recommendation engine that finds qualifying patients

Scanning EMR records, deciding who qualifies, or ranking trials is patient-specific clinical decision support and remains outside the project boundary.

What “candidate review” means

Plain language boundary

Trial Relay may help people review

Show exact criteria, open source records, highlight missing information, track the human review, and route a packet.

Trial Relay may show a trial-team outcome

After the authorised site decides, display its confirmed eligible/ineligible outcome with source and date.

Trial Relay may not decide who qualifies

No EMR-wide scan, inferred eligibility, recommendation score, “best trial,” or automatic patient list.
